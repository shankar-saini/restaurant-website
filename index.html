About the Restaurant

At Khana's Restaurant, we believe that vegetarian food can be delicious, healthy and full of amazing flavors. We prepare every dish using fresh vegetables, quality ingredients and carefully selected spices.

From traditional Indian recipes to modern vegetarian dishes, our goal is to provide fresh, flavorful and satisfying food for every guest.

Whether you are visiting with your family, friends or loved ones, Khana's Restaurant is the perfect place to enjoy a comfortable and delicious vegetarian meal.
